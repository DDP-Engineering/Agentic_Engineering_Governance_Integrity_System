  prevent inference from free-form logs and distinguish completion from the
  next stage, while the full log remains append-only (REQ-FUNC-1844e18d).
- Render ten fixed lines capped at 240 characters per value, mark omissions,
  and direct full-record review before continuing if output was shortened;
  missing/stale bindings cannot repeat an approval or success claim
  (REQ-FUNC-1844e18d).
- Reject duplicate or invalid records instead of silently choosing a value;
  blank legacy/scaffold state remains readable (REQ-FUNC-1844e18d).
- Keep the format in the task template and reminders in all ordinary/adoption
  step files; report once per boundary/pause, not per command, without new
  approval gates or suppressed deviation notices (REQ-UX-84868466).

  requirements and source; mandatory full validation/delivery reads remain
  intact, and one-stage sessions stop explicitly (REQ-UX-94a33d75).
- Rejected log-scraping and a model-backed summarizer because neither can
  reliably distinguish recorded fact from inference offline.
